Pico Recipes

A "Just Enough Items" (JEI) style item and recipe browser for Vintage Story. If you know JEI from Minecraft, you already know how to use it: a searchable list of every item and block in the game sits next to your inventory, and a couple of clicks (or a hover) shows you exactly how to craft anything — and what it is used for.

Features

  • Item list overlay — a searchable, paged panel of every item and block (including modded ones) that appears beside your inventory and disappears when you close it.
  • Hover to preview — hover an item and a compact recipe pops up right next to it, no click needed. If an item has several recipes, Shift + Mouse Wheel cycles through them.
  • Left click / R — see how to make an item in the full browser.
  • Right click / U — see what it is used for.
  • Drill down — click any ingredient or result inside a recipe to jump to its recipes, with a Back button to retrace your steps.
  • Search — a search bar at the bottom of the screen. Type any words to filter; use @modid to filter by mod (for example @game axe).
  • Every recipe type — grid crafting, smithing, clay forming, knapping, barrel mixing & aging, smelting / cooking, grinding and crushing. Voxel shapes (clay, knapping, smithing) are drawn as a small pixel grid.
  • Creative "cheat mode" — Shift + click an item in the list to give yourself one (creative mode only).
  • Toggle any time — press Ctrl + O to turn the whole overlay on or off; the choice is remembered.
  • The minimap is tucked away while the overlay is open, and shown again when it closes.

Controls

Ctrl + OEnable / disable the overlay
Left click / RShow recipes for the hovered item
Right click / UShow usages of the hovered item
Shift + Mouse WheelCycle recipes in the hover preview
Mouse WheelPage through the item list
Shift + clickGive the item (creative mode)

Multiplayer

Pico Recipes is a client-side mod — you can use it on any server. Installing it on the server as well simply enables the creative-mode "give item" feature; everything else works with the mod on the client only.

Requirements

Vintage Story 1.22.0 or newer.
